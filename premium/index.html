Questions

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free checks up to 200 words at a time, with 2 humanizes a day and no Citation Generator. Pro removes the word limit and unlocks unlimited humanizing and citations.

Is there a yearly plan?

Yes. Pro costs $39.99 USD a year, which works out to $3.33 a month, 33% less than paying monthly. Turn on Yearly above the plans before you click Upgrade.

How do I pay?

With PayPal or any debit or credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover). Click Upgrade, sign in with Google, and choose how to pay in the PayPal box.

How do I cancel?

Open your PayPal account, go to Settings, then Payments and Manage automatic payments, and cancel EssayWiz Pro. You keep Pro until the end of the month or year you already paid for.

Is my writing saved?

No, unless you tick the box to help train EssayWiz. See the privacy policy.
