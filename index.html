      Questions

      Is it free?

      Yes. Second Read is free for every student. You just sign in with Google; there's no payment or word paywall.

      How does the plagiarism check work?

      It picks your most distinctive sentences and searches the web and book sources such as Google Books, Project Gutenberg and the Internet Archive for the same or nearly the same wording, then links to each source it finds.

      How does the AI check work?

      Each sentence is read for signs of AI writing, like generic filler, stock phrases and a very even rhythm, and you get an overall AI-written percentage. No AI detector is perfect, so treat it as a guide for revising, not proof.

      Do I still need to cite sources after the rewrite?

      Yes. Putting an idea in your own words still means it came from someone else. The rewrite marks those places with a yellow [cite: …] reminder so you can add a proper reference.

      Can it check Word documents and PDFs?

      Yes. Upload a .docx, .pdf or .txt file and the text is read right in your browser.

      Is my essay saved?

      No. Your text is only used to produce your result and is not stored by this site.

      Why do I need to sign in?

      Signing in keeps the checker fair and free by limiting how many checks one person can run per minute. We only see your name and email.
