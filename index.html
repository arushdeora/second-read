Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The free plan checks up to 200 words at a time and includes 2 free humanizes a day. The Citation Generator is part of EssayWiz Pro. Pro removes the word limit for $5 USD a month, or $39.99 USD a year. You can pay with PayPal or any debit or credit card, and cancel any time.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open your PayPal account, go to Settings, then Payments and Manage automatic payments, and cancel EssayWiz Pro. You keep Pro until the end of the month you already paid for.

How does the plagiarism check work?

It picks your most distinctive sentences and searches the web and book sources such as Google Books, Project Gutenberg and the Internet Archive for the same or nearly the same wording.

How accurate is the AI detector?

No AI detector is perfect. Treat the percentage as a guide for revising, not proof of who wrote something.

Do I still need to cite sources after paraphrasing?

Yes. Putting an idea in your own words still means it came from someone else. Rewrites mark those places with a yellow [cite: …] reminder.

Is my text saved?

Only if you tick the box to help train EssayWiz. Otherwise your text is used just to produce your result and is not stored. You can delete saved texts any time.
