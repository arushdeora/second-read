      How the score works

      Quick check runs in your browser and nothing leaves the page. It measures how much your sentence lengths vary, how often you use phrases common in AI text ("delve", "it is important to note"), and how personal the voice is (contractions, "I").

      Analyze with AI sends the text to Claude (Anthropic's AI model), which reads each sentence and explains what feels generated. The final score blends both, 70% AI and 30% statistics. Your text is used only to produce the result and is not stored by this site.

      If a sentence is flagged and you wrote it, that is common. Adding your own examples, opinions, and specific sources usually makes writing read as more human, and better.
