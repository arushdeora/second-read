# EssayWiz facts for the Instagram agent

Only claim what is written here. Update this file whenever the site changes.

- Website: essaywiz.app (link in bio). Instagram: @essaywiz.app
- Who it's for: high-school and university students writing essays, reports and assignments.
- Tools: Essay Check (plagiarism %, AI-written % and grammar in one check), AI Detector (sentence by sentence),
  Plagiarism Checker (compares with web sources and shows where matches came from), Grammar Checker (explains each fix),
  Paraphraser (8 modes: standard, fluency, formal, academic, simple, creative, shorten, expand),
  Humanizer (rewrites stiff, robotic-sounding text so it reads naturally), Citation Generator (APA, MLA, Chicago; Pro),
  Proofread (a writing-history report that shows an essay is the student's own work; Pro).
- Free plan: up to 200 words per check, 2 humanizes a day. Sign in with Google. No credit card needed.
- Pro: no word limit, unlimited humanizing, Citation Generator, Proofread. Prices differ by country, so never state a price
  on Instagram: say "free to start" or "see the price on essaywiz.app".
- India: students pay in rupees with UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, RuPay or Indian cards.
- Privacy: essays are not saved unless the student opts in to help train EssayWiz.
- Created by Arush Deora.

## Rules for every post
- Be honest. AI detectors can be wrong; never promise a result, a grade, or that work will "pass" any checker.
- Never say: undetectable, bypass, beat/fool/trick Turnitin or any detector, 100% accurate, guaranteed.
- Never encourage handing in work that isn't the student's own. Position tools as checking and improving YOUR writing.
- No prices, no discounts or offers that don't exist, no fake reviews or user counts.
