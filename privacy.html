Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Second Read is a free tool that helps students check their own writing for AI-like sentences and copied text. This page explains what we collect and why.

Google sign-in

To use the checker you sign in with your Google account. Google shares only your basic profile with us: your name, email address and profile picture. We use this only to show who is signed in and to limit how many checks one person can run per minute, so the tool stays free and fair. We do not post anything to your account, read your email, or access any other Google data.

Your text

When you run a check, your text is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI model to produce the result, and for the plagiarism check, to search the web. Your text is not stored by Second Read and is not used to build any profile of you. The quick check runs entirely in your browser.

Files you upload

Word, PDF and text files are read inside your browser. Only the extracted text is used, as described above.

Cookies and storage

We keep your sign-in in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information. Text is processed by Anthropic only to produce your result, under Anthropic's terms.

Deleting your data

Click Sign out on the site to remove your sign-in from the browser. You can also remove Second Read's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
